About Us

Wheels4U is a Vehicle Sharing & Rental Platform designed to make transportation simple, affordable, and convenient. Our platform connects vehicle owners with people looking to rent cars, bikes, or scooters for their daily travel or special trips.

💡 Our Mission

To make vehicle rentals simple, affordable, and accessible for everyone.

🚀 Our Vision

Our goal is to make better use of existing vehicles by connecting owners with people who need them.
